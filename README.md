Chuckwood vs. Thickwood: Battle of the Gangster Groundhogs — Al Capone's Death Star

Chuckwood vs. Thickwood: Battle of the Gangster Groundhogs — Al Capone's Death Star (CVTBGGACDS) is a simple narrative-based game made using the Unity Engine for the 2025 Game Maker's Toolkit (GMTK) Game Jam by a team of four developers and a musical contributor. A browser-playable version of the project can be found at [https://sadless.itch.io/edwin-ii-skyward](https://sadless.itch.io/chuckwood-vs-thickwood).

In CVTBGGACDS, the player takes on the roll of a groundhog telephone operator who is tasked with connecting the telephone calls of various actors in the city of New Dobren, all while tapping the wires and listening in on various conversations to ultimately prevent the assassination of notorious mob boss Acron Gnut. The theme of the 2025 GMTK Game Jam was "Loop," and CVTBGGACDS attempted to emphasize this theme both through the looping "Groundhog Day"-like structure of the narrative, and in the way that the player must use their wire-tapping skills to stay "in the loop" to solve the ultimate mystery at the heart of the story.

The player must simultaneously keep up with their tasks as a telephone operator while also ensuring they listen into as many conversations as they can to get the most information possible. Because many important conversations run simultaneously, the player must play through the day multiple times, insanity looming larger with each passing cycle, to supply their boss with enough information to prevent the assassination at the end of the day.

The project used jsfxr for sound effect generation and sources its font to Bitstream, found at https://www.fontspace.com/bitstream-vera-sans-mono-font-f13233.
